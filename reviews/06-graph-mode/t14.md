The sixth defect a live run found, and the shortest-lived: T1 of spec 07
was resumed, and the resume executed the runner written the night before
— by a recipe with no --verbose, no stream-json, and no check for a
finished turn, all three of which had been fixed hours earlier. The task
went back to work carrying every defect we had spent the day removing.

The spec said "re-runs the task's runner", and the implementation was
faithful to it. The words were wrong: a runner is generated code, not a
record of the run, and it ages with the recipe that wrote it. A resume
re-runs the *task* — the worktree, the branch and the log are what it
keeps.
