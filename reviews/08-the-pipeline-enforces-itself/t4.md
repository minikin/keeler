## Findings (settled)

1. **The `Status:` grammar was stricter than the one the pipeline
   writes.** `keeler-land` finds the line with `^[*_[:space:]]*Status:`
   (Justfile:1281, 1296) and rewrites Approved to Implemented in place,
   whatever decorates it; the reader demanded `**Status:**` at column
   zero. A spec written `Status: Approved` would be marked Implemented by
   the pipeline and read as unfinished by the gate — `unkept_promises`
   returning nothing for exactly the spec whose promise needed checking.
   The module's own header says the two grammars must agree. Fixed
   test-first in 5dac5ab: `status_value` strips the same decoration the
   Justfile's `sed` does, pinned by
   `the_status_line_is_read_however_it_is_decorated`.

2. **An indented checkbox is dropped, not refused.** `  - [x] **T2 —
   Child.**` inside the Tasks section is neither read as a task nor
   refused. Not changed, deliberately: `scripts/keeler-graph.sh` anchors
   items at column zero too and reads an indented line as a continuation
   of the item above — which is what the wrapped continuation lines in
   this very spec's Tasks section are. Refusing here would make the two
   grammars disagree, and agreement is the property the module is built
   on. The header now says so rather than implying the refusal is total.
