## An ambiguity in the spec, resolved and reported

The bands paragraph reads "below that columns keep dropping right to left
— CTX, then COMMIT". Those two say different things: COMMIT is the
rightmost of the pair, so "right to left" would take it first, while the
list names CTX first. **The named order is what is implemented** — the
percentage goes, then the commit facts — on the grounds that the ordered
list is the more specific of the two statements, and that the ladder above
it is not strictly right-to-left either (MODEL is dropped with TOKENS,
which stands four columns to its right). The 60-column scenario passes
under either reading, so no test tells them apart. **If the other reading
was meant, the fix is one line in `layout::LADDER` and its unit test.**

diff. It checked the band arithmetic against the spec's table by hand and

1. **The first header line measured only its left half.** `first` fitted the

dropped whole rather than clipped, which is the change working.
